\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Composite \doxylink{classgnode_1_1Node}{Node} that encapsulates an entire child \doxylink{classgnode_1_1Graph}{Graph}. 

Enables hierarchical graph composition (\doxylink{classgnode_1_1Graph}{Graph} of Graphs). Boundary inputs (\doxylink{classgnode_1_1NodeIn}{Node\+In}) and boundary outputs (\doxylink{classgnode_1_1NodeOut}{Node\+Out}) in the child graph can be registered as external ports of this node.
